# func_8001D63C

Per-frame update of a game object: steering/acceleration from the camera angle (parameter blocks picked by the object's
state flags), the ring copy of the player's position history, turn smoothing via `func_8001E3DC`/`func_8001E544`,
`func_8001A4A4`/`func_8001AA80`, velocity integration with collision (`func_80029884`), floor probe, velocity clamp,
carrying a parent object, gravity and the proximity check (`func_800310EC`, `func_8002C324`).

Written from the m2c draft and checked against `target.s` (fields accessed with `FIELD()` macros over byte offsets).

Lessons: helpers must be `static inline` (a real `jal` shows up as an extra call); signed `/ 1024` is spelled
`scale_div` (the retail rounds negatives toward zero); `func_8003D360` is called before `D_80063450` is read.

Result: `best.c` (= `attempt1.c`) agrees on 482 of 500 (seed 0), 480 of 500 (seed 1), 1926 of 2000 (seed 2).

leaves leftover values in `$a0`-`$a2` (`$a0` = the x step, `$a2` = often `D_80063450`, `$a1` unexplained), which
the tester compares. Passing `sp18[0]` and others through an implicit declaration (`attempt2.c`) fixes `$a0` only (483 of 500)
and is not behaviour, so `best.c` keeps the clean call. No other kind of disagreement was seen among the first
disagreements; the rest were not individually inspected.
