# func_8001A710

Pairs two objects (`arg0`, `arg2`) through their link field (+0x58). When `arg0` is already linked to another object,
the conflict is resolved by distance (`func_80030FEC` on +0x60) and flag 0x800 at +0x100; the losing case returns -0x1C.
Then tests the boxes `arg1`/`arg3` (grown/shrunk by each object's +0x128) against each other with `func_80030EC0`,
setting bit 0x1000 in +0xA8 on a hit. Returns -0xFF.

Best: `best.c` (= `earlier.c`), 8 of 127 words differ. Control flow, registers and every instruction past the prologue
match; retail places `addu $s5,$a1` after `lw $s2,0x58($s1)`, the compile hoists it (and the `$s5` save) above the `$ra`
store.
