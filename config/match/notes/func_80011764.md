# func_80011764

A `calloc`: allocates `count * size` bytes with `func_8001148C`, zeroes them with `bzero` and returns the block, or 0
when the allocation fails.

## Result

Not matched. `best.c` (attempt1) has the target's body and control flow exactly (beqz to the `return 0` path, `j` over
it); the score reads 19 of 22 because every word after the prologue is shifted by one.

What differs is the prologue's schedule only. Retail: `mult; addiu sp; sw s1; mflo s1; nop; sw ra; sw s0`. GCC 2.7.2
gives `mult; addiu sp; sw ra; sw s1; sw s0; mflo s1`, with no `nop`. The retail `nop` after `mflo` and the `mflo` placed
right after the `$s1` save point to a different scheduling (or assembler hazard rule) than the one reproduced here.
