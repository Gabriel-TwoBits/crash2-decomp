# func_80028C48

Picks the entity a position (`arg1` = x, y, z) lands on, and reports it through `func_8002890C`.
Requires bit 0x04000000 in `arg0->0xAC`; otherwise returns 0.

1. Scans the entity list at `D_800608CC->0x10`; the first active entry for which `func_80030D74` agrees goes to
   `func_8003DA00` with its range scaled by 256 (six stack arguments, hence the cast prototype), retrying until a result
   or no entry matches (then null).
2. Scans the `D_8006CB6C` slot list (+0x290): a slot inside the x/z box (margin 0x88B8) whose y is in range is taken at
   once (`arg1[1]` = its y); otherwise the nearest candidate below the old y is kept and adopted at the end if nothing
   better exists.
3. Calls `func_8002890C(arg0->0x44, ...)`: (-1, `res->0x118`) if adopted from step 2, ((res & 0x3C00) >> 10, -1) otherwise,
   (0, -1) with no result.

Result: 500 of 500 trials agree on seeds 0 and 1 (equivalent.c).
