# func_8001E640

Interpolates a sine-table lookup (func_8003D2EC) over a period `arg1`: phase = (arg0 & 0xFFF) % arg1 scaled to 11 bits,
sine scaled by `arg2`, clamped to +-0x1000, rescaled by `arg1` (shifted by 2 when `arg1 > 0xFFFF` to avoid overflow),
added to the whole-period part `(phase / arg1) * arg1`.

- m2c's draft: 17 of 80 words differ; control flow and the first 0xFC bytes match.
- Tail split into separate statements (`prod`, `base`), either operand order: 16 of 80.
- decomp-permuter reported score 0 on `best.c`'s variant (reuses `temp_v1` for the final shift), but `score.sh` still says
  16 of 80, so it is not a match.

and shifts it after the second `mult` is issued; the compiled code uses `$a0`, no nops, and shifts earlier.
