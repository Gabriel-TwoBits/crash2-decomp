Projects a world position (`arg0`: three s32) to screen space with the GTE. The position minus the camera
(`D_800607F4/F8/FC`) is shifted right by 8 into an s16 vector, `RotTransPers` projects it (rotation `D_80060774`,
translation `D_80060130`), and `arg1` receives `(x - 0x100) << 8`, `(0x6C - y) << 8` and the depth from
`ReadSZfifo3` shifted left by 8. Returns -0xFF.

The logic in `best.c` (= `attempt2.c`) follows every instruction of the target. Final count: 127 of 500 trials agree
(seed 0), 136 of 500 (seed 1).

locals from `sp+0x10`; the compiler starts locals at `sp+0x18`, so reproducing the retail slots (`sz[]` at `sp+0x34`) needs
8 bytes of padding that grows the frame to 0x58, and the saved `s0` word that the retail `$a2` window reaches (`sp+0x48`)
sits elsewhere. Return values, call targets and arguments, and every changed memory word agree.
