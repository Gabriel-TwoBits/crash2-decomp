# func_80017BC4

Per-frame camera update. Builds the view matrix at D_800607F4-0xA0 from the three camera angles
(D_80060808 roll, D_80060800 pitch, D_80060804 yaw), sets the far color and translation, stores the
mirrored matrix (func_80017AF8) and a copy at D_80060794. Depending on bit 0x1000 of a flag in the
object at D_800608CC->0x10 it either builds a tilted matrix with a bobbing offset (D_80060840) or
copies 0x40 bytes of matrices forward by 0x60 bytes. Then sets the GTE offset, decays the shake in D_8006CB78,
saves the camera position and angles, picks the heading D_80060914 and its sin/cos, and calls func_80018294.

Notes: m2c read the first word of the 0x40-byte matrix copy as an s16; the assembly is a plain `lw`.

Result: attempt1.c = equivalent.c, 500 of 500 trials agree on seeds 0 and 1 (about 380 end in the same exception).
