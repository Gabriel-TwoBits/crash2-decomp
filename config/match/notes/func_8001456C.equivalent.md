# func_8001456C

Returns `D_800675B0.count` minus the number of entries (0x2C bytes each) in `D_80067950` and in `D_800675B0` whose
`state` (s16 at +4) is not 1 and whose `f_f` (s8 at +0xF) is nonzero. Both tables are walked in full by their own counts.

Single attempt, `equivalent.c`: 500 of 500 trials agree on seed 0 and on seed 1. Nothing still disagrees.
Not written for byte matching (the matching loop gave it up, see EARLIER.md).
