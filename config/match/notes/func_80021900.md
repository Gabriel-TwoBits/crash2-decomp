# func_80021900

Walks `count` entries of a u32 array at `p`. An entry with bit 0 set indexes a table (`D_80067590`, offset `(v >> 13) & 0x3FC`) giving a
record pointer; the pointer is kept if it is higher than the previous one, then advanced in 8-byte steps until the word at +4 equals
the entry. Otherwise the entry itself is the pointer. With `flags & 8` it calls `func_80014260(cur, flags | 2, arg3)`, else
`func_8001434C(cur, flags & 4 ? -arg3 : arg3)`.

Matched: 0 of 66 words differ (`score.sh`). attempt1 (plain C) was 1 off, the `andi` in the delay slot of the jump after the search loop;
decomp-permuter found the `cur -= 2; cur += 2;` form after the `while` (net zero, only steers the scheduler).
