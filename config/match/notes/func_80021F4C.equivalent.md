# func_80021F4C

Camera-angle update. Takes the angle from the camera target (`D_8005BB3C`/`D_8005BB44`) to the position in `arg0`
(+0x800, masked to 12 bits), clamps it (0x201..0x800 -> 0x200, 0x801..0xDFF -> 0xE00), stores it in `D_80060914`,
smooths it with `func_800312CC` against `D_8005B9AC` (0x1001 means unset; `arg1 != 0` resets it), then compares it with
the angle toward `D_80060818`/`D_80060820`. The wrapped difference outside [-2, 2] sets `D_800608F8` to 1 (negative) or
2; inside, to 0. With a nonzero direction it scales |diff| by 221*16>>12 and calls `func_80024268`; when that and
`func_80026764` succeed it takes `D_800608F8` from the second result and re-runs `func_80024268`. Ends with
`func_800200A8` and `func_800231B0(position copy)`.
