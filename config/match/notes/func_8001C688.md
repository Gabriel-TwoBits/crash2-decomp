# func_8001C688

Reads a record pointer at +0xC4 of the object, stores cursor (+0xBC) and a word (+0xC0), optionally merges the low 16 bits of
the first word into `*arg1`, returns -0x19 when the record's upper half-word is 0, otherwise advances the cursor (+0xBC) and the
record pointer (+0xC4) to `arg0 + 0x40 + {low, high} half-word` and returns -0xFF.

Final count (best.c = attempt8.c): 2 of 36 words differ, both `addu` operand order in the last two address computations
(retail `addu v1, v1, a0`; compiled `addu v1, a0, v1`).

Findings:
- The reload of +0xBC after its store needs a volatile read (`*(u32 * volatile *)`); otherwise it is forwarded.
- Loading `v1b[-1]` into a temporary before the `if (arg1)` reproduces the hoisted `lw a2`.
- `arg0` as `u8 *` keeps it in `$a0`; casting it to `u32` (attempt2, attempt7, attempt10) copies it to `$a2` (34-36 words differ).
- `&arg0[x + 0x40]` and `x + 0x40 + arg0` give the same swapped `addu`.
