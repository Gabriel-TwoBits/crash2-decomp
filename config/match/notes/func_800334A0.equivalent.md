# func_800334A0

Per-frame sound mixer update. Applies the master/reverb volume changes, ramps the master volume and starts a fade
command on the music sequence when `D_8005F18C` is 1 or -1. Then for each voice entry (`D_8005F4EC`, 0x50 bytes) from
`D_8005F304` to 0x17 that is active: resolves the sample source (resource, queue handle or queued request) and starts
the voice, counts down the start delay, advances the volume and pitch ramps, follows the owning object's position and
sends the SPU command (`func_800518B8`). Stopped entries are released with `func_80033448`.

Attempts: `attempt1.c` passed 500 of 500 on seed 0, but a seed-1 trial disagreed on the second argument of
`func_80032A98` (`&tag`, not the handle). `attempt2.c` fixed it.

Final count (`equivalent.c`): 500 of 500 on seeds 0 and 1, 3000 of 3000 on seed 2. Most trials end in the same
exception on both sides (about 80%), so memory is compared only on the rest.
