# func_8002CC50 notes

Rasterises a transformed box into a 32-row `[min, max]` byte table and ORs the covered cells into the bitmap at `D_8005F3C4`.

- Builds the 8 box corners from `arg0`, transforms them with `func_8003EAD4`.
- Up to three levels (`arg1[1]`, midpoint, `arg1[4]`, clamped to `arg0+0x30`/`0x3C`) are clipped with `func_8002FFFC`; each gives `n` edges.
- The bounding box is converted to a 32x32 grid (origin `arg2`, `(d*4)/8192 + 16`); boxes off the grid are skipped.
- Each edge is walked with Bresenham (horizontal, x-major, z-major) into the row table. With `arg3 != 0` it also calls
  `func_8002CB1C` on cells in a -16..47 window, one per 8 steps, plus a pending last cell.
- Each row's span is OR-ed into the bitmap as a mask.
- The return value is the leftover `$v0` of the retail code. Each early exit leaves the value its branch delay slot computed
  (e.g. the `arg3 != 0` rejects return `hiZ + 32`, `loX < 32`, `loZ < 32`, `0`), reproduced by `ret`.

## Result

`best.c`: 496 of 500 trials agree on seeds 0 and 1. Not equivalent.c (landing needs every trial).

What raised the count from 421 (earlier.c):

- The two `static` helpers were real calls under GCC 2.7.2 (the tester counted them as extra calls out): now `static inline`.
  Landing may refuse `inline`; expanding the helpers by hand is the fallback.
- Early-exit `$v0` values (delay slots), above.
- The locals the retail code keeps in the frame (`corners`, `pts`, `tbl`, `arg2`, `arg3`, the three levels, the pending row,
  the table pointer) sit in one struct at the retail offsets, with trailing padding sized to the retail frame, because
  `func_8002FFFC` isn't run and `pts` is whatever the stack holds: the word values the points read depend on the frame
  address. Padding of 7 words gives 400 trials, 9 gives 486, 10 or 11 give 496, 12 or 13 give 400.
  The locals are `#define`d onto the struct fields in `best.c` (working-file hack; needs rewriting as `f.x` before landing).
