# func_8002CC50 notes

Rasterises a transformed box into a 32-row `[min, max]` byte table and ORs the covered cells into the bitmap at `D_8005F3C4`.

- Builds the 8 box corners from `arg0`, transforms them with `func_8003EAD4`.
- Up to three levels (`arg1[1]`, midpoint, `arg1[4]`, clamped to `arg0+0x30`/`0x3C`) are clipped with `func_8002FFFC`;
  each gives `n` edges.
- The bounding box is converted to a 32x32 grid (origin `arg2`); boxes off the grid are skipped.
- Each edge is walked with Bresenham (horizontal, x-major, z-major) into the row table. With `arg3 != 0` it also calls
  `func_8002CB1C` on cells in a -16..47 window, one per 8 steps, plus a pending last cell.
- Each row's span is OR-ed into the bitmap as a mask.
- The return value is the leftover `$v0` of the retail code; early exits reproduce the delay-slot values.

## What still disagrees

With a large made-up `n` from `func_8002FFFC`, the min/max and edge loops read `pts` past its 12 points into the rest of
the frame: retail's spill slots (`0x148` `arg2`, `0x150` `arg3`, `0x158..0x168` the levels, `0x170` pending z, `0x178`
`&tbl`, `0x180..0x19C` the registers saved around `func_8002CB1C`). The candidate's own spill slots hold other values
there, so those trials end differently (seed 1 trial 427, seed 2 trial 340: call 21 to `func_8002CB1C`). Mirroring
them needs a frame with no spill slot of GCC's own, which the C doesn't control.

(identical text to the compiler).
