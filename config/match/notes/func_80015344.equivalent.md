# func_80015344

Fills a table of 0x2C-byte entries at D_80062BB8. Arguments: unused, y offset, total length, step, count.
- Rows = ceil(total / 65424). Going downward from the last row, each gets kind 4; the last row's y drops by `total % 65424` rounded up to a multiple of 16, the others by 65424. y is stored at +0x18. D_80067FB8 gets the final y.
- Then abs(count) extra rows (kind 5, constant 0x6396347F at +0x14), y dropping by `step` each; for a negative count y is restored afterwards.
- Remaining rows up to 10 get kind 3; mode 0x1E when y - 0x10000 < 0x1010, otherwise mode 1 and y advances.

Result: seed 0 agrees on 500 of 500 trials (equivalent.c). Seeds 1, 2 and 3 stop at a trial where the emulator (Unicorn) raises reserved instruction on the retail code, so they are unsupported rather than disagreeing; the two-seed criterion is not met.
