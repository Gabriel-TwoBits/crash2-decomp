# func_80015344

Fills the 0x2C-byte entry table at D_80062BB8. Arguments: unused, y offset, total length, step, count (stack).
- Rows = ceil(total / 65424). From the last row down, each gets kind 4; the last row's y drops by `total % 65424` rounded up to a multiple of 16, the others by 65424. y is stored at +0x18. D_80067FB8 gets the final y.
- Then abs(count) extra rows (kind 5, constant 0x6396347F at +0x14), y dropping by `step`; for a negative count y is restored afterwards.
- Remaining rows up to 10 get kind 3: mode 0x1E when y - 0x10000 < 0x1010, otherwise mode 1 and y advances.

Result: the earlier draft (unchanged) agrees on 500 of 500 trials on seeds 0 and 1; 1998 of 2000 with `--trials 2000`.
The 2 disagreements (trials 637 and 1553) have total ~0x69000000 (~27000 rows): the retail first loop
exceeds the 500000-instruction limit while the candidate's cheaper loop finishes under it. A difference in
instruction count, not in behaviour; every branch of the assembly has its counterpart in the C.
