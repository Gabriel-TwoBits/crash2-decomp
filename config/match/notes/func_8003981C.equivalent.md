# func_8003981C notes

Condition evaluator of a MIPS-interpreter step (`arg0` context: registers at +0x40, `$sp` at +0xBC, `$pc` at +0xC0).
Bits 20-21 pick the condition (0 always, 1 register nonzero, 2 register zero; register 31 pre-decrements `$sp` and reads
the stack). Bits 22-23 pick the action: 1 pops N words into `arg3[3..]` and calls `func_8001C688`, 2 calls it, 0 adjusts
`$pc`/`$sp` when the condition is false.

Mode 3 leaves `$t1` unset in retail; the C takes the condition as true, and equiv.py leaves those trials uncompared.

Final: `equivalent.c` (the earlier run's C). 500/500 (seeds 0, 1), 2000/2000 (seeds 1, 2).
