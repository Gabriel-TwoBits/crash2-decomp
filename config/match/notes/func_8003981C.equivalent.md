# func_8003981C

Condition evaluator of a MIPS-interpreter step. `arg0` is the context (registers at +0x40, `$sp` at +0xBC, `$pc` at +0xC0).
Instruction bits 20-21 pick the condition (0 always, 1 register value, 2 register is zero; register 31 pre-decrements `$sp`
and reads the stack), bits 22-23 the action when taken (1 pops N words into `arg3[3..]` and calls `func_8001C688`, 2 calls it,
0 adjusts `$pc`/`$sp` when the condition fails).

Final count: 480/500 (seed 0), 475/500 (seed 1), `best.c` (= `attempt2.c`).

the C treats it as false. Not reproducible in plain C, hence not `equivalent.c`.
