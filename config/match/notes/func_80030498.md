# func_80030498

Walks the 4 vertices (x, y, z; 12 bytes each) of a quad, finds the first two edges that cross height `arg1` in z,
interpolates x/y on each crossing (12-bit fixed point, divisor forced to 1 when 0), then interpolates y at x = `arg0`
between the two crossings. Returns 0xC4653601 when fewer than two edges cross.

Matched: 0 of 122 words differ (attempt6.c). Needed: `goto`-built crossing test copying m2c's branch shape, `d1` computed
before `d0` (load order), and the final quotient in its own temporary.
