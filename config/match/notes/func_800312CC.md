# func_800312CC

Interpolates between two 12-bit angles: the difference `arg1 - arg0` is sign-extended from 12 bits,
scaled by `arg2` (8-bit fraction), and added to `arg0`; the result is masked to 12 bits.

m2c's draft matched unchanged on the first score (0 of 9 words differ). `match.c` is the draft without `m2c_macros.h`.
