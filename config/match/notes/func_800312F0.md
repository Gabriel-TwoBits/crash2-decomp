# func_800312F0

Wraps a 12-bit angle step: `d` is the signed 12-bit difference `arg1 - arg0`, clamped to `[-arg3, arg3]`;
returns `(arg0 + ((arg2 * d) >> 8)) & 0xFFF`.

Tried: nested if/else clamp (16 words off), m2c's `||` chain (9 off; the dead `a1 = a3` copy was dropped).
Matched (0 of 20 words differ) with a `goto` into the second clamp's body, which is the shape of the original's shared clamp block.
