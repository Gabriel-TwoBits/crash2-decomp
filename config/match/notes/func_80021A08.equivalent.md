distance, flips a "near" latch (`D_8005B99A`) on two distance thresholds, and blends the angle through
`func_800312CC` (latched angle, sway, zoom blend, smoothing against the previous angle `D_8005B9AC`). The wrapped
difference to the angle toward `D_80060818/D_80060820` gives a turn direction (`D_800608F8`) and a step count; the
position is stepped with `func_80024268`/`func_80026764` and passed to `func_800200A8`. The tail adds a
distance-based vector from `func_8002FF54` to `D_800607F4..FC`.

the trials compare the frame words around the `func_80024268` output pointer, so a larger `sp50` disagreed.
