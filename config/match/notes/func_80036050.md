# func_80036050

Clears byte 0x34 of each of the 15 entries (stride 0x4C) of the table pointed to by `D_8005F264`, skipping the
pass when the pointer is NULL (re-read every iteration).

Attempts: m2c draft with a `do/while` and a separate offset counter gave 8 differing words (loop inverted by GCC);
a `for` with `i * 0x4C` gave 1 (operand order of the `addu`); the permuter closed it by assigning the address to a
temporary `s8 *` (`(i * 0x4C) + (s8 *)D_8005F264 + 0x34`). Final: 0 of 13 words differ.
