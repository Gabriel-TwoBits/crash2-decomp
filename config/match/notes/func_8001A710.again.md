# func_8001A710

Pairs two objects (`arg0`, `arg2`) through their link field (+0x58). When `arg0` is already linked to another object,
the conflict is resolved by `Math_ApproxDistance` on +0x60 and flag 0x800 at +0x100; the losing case returns -0x1C.
Then tests the boxes `arg1`/`arg3` (grown/shrunk by each object's +0x128) against each other with `func_80030EC0`,
setting bit 0x1000 in +0xA8 on a hit. Returns -0xFF.

Best: `best.c` (`earlier.c` with its conflicting `func_80030EC0` prototype dropped), 8 of 127 words differ, 2 of 127
without a counterpart. Only the prologue differs: retail places `addu $s5,$a1` after `lw $s2,0x58($s1)`; the compile
hoists it (and the `$s5` save) above the `$ra` store.
