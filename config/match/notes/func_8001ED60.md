# func_8001ED60

Converts a world position (`arg0`, three s32) to screen-relative values in `arg1`: loads the rotation and translation
matrices (`D_80060774`, `D_80060130`), subtracts the offsets `D_800607F4/F8/FC`, shifts by 8 into an SVECTOR, runs
`RotTransPers`, then writes `(x-0x100)<<8`, `(0x6C-y)<<8` and the depth from `ReadSZfifo3` `<<8`. Returns -0xFF.

Not matched. Closest: `best.c`, 43 of 65 words differ (score.sh), mostly the stack frame and scheduling.
Tried:
- screen xy as `s32` and as `s16` read through an `s32` cast: GCC still emits `lh 0x2A` for y instead of `lw`+`sra 16`.
- frame is 0x48 against 0x50: padding arrays moved the other locals (pad before `sp10`, after `sz2`, `sz[5]`) without
  reaching the retail layout (sp10@0x10, sp18@0x18, xy@0x28, SZ@0x34..0x3C).
- Retail loads the three globals into t0/t1/t2 early; mine loads them into v1/a3/t0 lazily. Statement order of the three
  `sp10[]` stores (z,x,y and x,y,z) did not change it.
