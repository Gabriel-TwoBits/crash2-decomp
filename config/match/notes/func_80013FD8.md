# func_80013FD8

Retries `func_80013F5C(a0, a3, a1, a2)` until it returns something other than -0xFF (then returns 1). If `a1 & 1` and the
u16 at +4 is not 0x14 or 0x15, calls `func_80012BC4(a0)`, zeroes the last argument and retries; otherwise returns the s8 at +0xF.

Matched (0 of 40 words differ) on attempt 2. A `for (;;)` loop (attempt 1) hoisted `a1 & 1` and -0xFF into saved registers (38 differ); a `goto` loop fixed it.
