through `func_80036E30(arg0, arg1 & 0xFFF, 0)`. Ops 1/6 store `func_80014260(src, 0|1, 1)` at +0xD4; op 2 stores
`func_800143C0(src, 1)` at +0xD4; ops 3/4 push `func_800143C0(src, 0)` / `func_8001456C()` on the stack at +0xBC
(4 bytes); op 5 calls `func_80014918(sp - n*4, n)` with n = *src and pushes the result at the lowered pointer. Other ops: no-op.
