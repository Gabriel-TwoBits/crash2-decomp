Not matched: best.c (attempt1.c, the landed equivalent C) is at 2 of 155 words differ (1 instruction without a
counterpart, registers aside). Same score as the first pass.

What it does: clears `inner->f28`; for a non-null `anim` whose first byte isn't 4, opens the kseg0-aligned entry at
`anim + 4` into `f28` and bails out (f28 = 0) unless it is below -0xFE. For `*anim == 1` it stores
`func_8001A90C(obj, &p, &inner->f24)` in `f2C`, `p` in `f20`, `Nsf_OpenEntry(p + 0x10, 1, 0)` in `f28`, and for
`g_Player` scales six sums of `p`'s fields by `|arg1[0..2]|` into `f30[0..5]`. Otherwise `f28 = -0xE`.

Remaining difference: retail loads `p` (0x28($sp), v0) before `inner` (0x8($s0), v1) at 0xBC/0xC0; ours swaps them.
