# func_8001D22C
Copies `arg2` words from `arg3` to the buffer at `arg0+0xBC` (advancing that pointer by 4 per word), calls
`func_8001C620(arg0, arg2, 0xFFFF)`, sets `arg0+0xC0` to the table at `(*(arg0+0xC))+0x14` plus `(arg1 & 0x3FFF) * 4`,
then returns `func_8003A014(arg0, 3, 0)`.
m2c's draft scaled the pointer steps by 16 (s32* + 4); the assembly steps 4 bytes.
Result: 500 of 500 trials agree on seeds 0 and 1 (equivalent.c).
