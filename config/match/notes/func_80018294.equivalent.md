# func_80018294

Fills an array of 0x30-byte entries (count at arg0[0]; entry i at arg0 + 0x30 * i) for each bit i set in the low byte of func_800321D4's result.
Each active entry gets a source record from func_80014260(arg0 + 4 + 0x30 * i, 1, 0). Its shape (src+0x10) supplies a position relative to the camera (D_800607F4/F8/FC >> 8; F8 shifts logically), or zeros when shape+0xC == 1. The other fields are copied from the source.

m2c's draft advanced its pointers by 0x30 words instead of bytes; fixed.
Result: 500 of 500 trials agree on seeds 0 and 1 (equivalent.c).
